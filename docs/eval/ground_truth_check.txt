Q1: What is the punishment for theft under IPC?
GROUND TRUTH:
Under Section 379 of the Indian Penal Code, 1860, whoever commits theft shall be punished with imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to three years, or with fine, or with both.

Q2: What is maternity leave duration in India?
GROUND TRUTH:
Under the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961, a woman is entitled to 26 weeks of maternity leave for her first two children. For a woman with two or more surviving children, the entitlement is 12 weeks, of which not more than 6 weeks may be taken before the expected delivery date.

Q3: What is gratuity and who is eligible for it?
GROUND TRUTH:
Gratuity is a lump-sum payment made by an employer to an employee under the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, as a reward for continuous service. An employee is eligible after completing at least 5 years of continuous service with the same employer, typically paid on retirement, resignation, or death.

Q4: Suppose I am arrested without a warrant — is that legal?
GROUND TRUTH:
Under Sections 41 and 41A of the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973, police may arrest without a warrant in specific circumstances, such as when a person is involved in a cognizable offence, with reasonable grounds. The police must inform the person of the grounds for arrest and their right to bail where applicable.

Q5: What are the key differences between a cognizable and non-cognizable offense in India?
GROUND TRUTH:
Under the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973, a cognizable offense allows police to arrest without a warrant and begin investigation without prior court permission. A non-cognizable offense requires a warrant for arrest and police cannot investigate without a magistrate's permission.
